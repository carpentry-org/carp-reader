(Fn [Form] ())

(Fn [(Ref Form a)] String)

str

defn

(Fn [(Ref Form a)] String) 

                        (str a)

renders an Form back to source-like text. Round-trips for syntactic content; whitespace around comments is left to the caller's formatting pass.
